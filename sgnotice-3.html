📚 ACADEMICS

Preparation for End-of-Term Assessments

Begin preparing early for class tests, assignments and examinations.

  • Attend lessons regularly.
  • Complete assignments on time.
  • Revise class notes.
  • Ask teachers for help when needed.
  • Avoid examination malpractice.

Arrive at examination venues early and bring only materials permitted by the examination authorities. Cheating and other examination malpractice may be dealt with under school and examination regulations.
